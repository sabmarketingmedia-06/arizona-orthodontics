Braces for Adults: Modern Options That Fit Your Life

We understand that adults have unique goals and responsibilities, so our orthodontic solutions are tailored to work around your lifestyle. At our Phoenix and Mesa locations, we offer several highly effective and low-profile options:

Charm Braces

Looking for something different? Our stylish Charm Braces offer a beautiful champagne-gold appearance, giving adults a unique way to express themselves while straightening their teeth.

Traditional Metal Braces

For more complex cases or when maximum efficiency is needed, metal braces remain a tried-and-true solution. Today's versions are smaller, more comfortable, and more effective than ever before.

Customized Care for Adult Smiles

Because your jaw and facial structures are fully developed, adult orthodontic treatment can require a different approach than what we use for younger patients. But don't worry. Our experienced team specializes in crafting personalized treatment plans that meet your needs, your goals, and your lifestyle. You'll receive care that's efficient, effective, and designed with your comfort in mind.

Why Straighten Your Teeth as an Adult?

Beyond cosmetics, orthodontic treatment offers real functional and health benefits at any age:

Improved Oral Health

Straighter teeth are easier to brush and floss, helping reduce the risk of cavities, gum disease, and other dental issues.

Boosted Confidence

A great smile can improve how you feel about yourself: at work, on dates, in photos, and in daily life.

Better Function

Orthodontics can correct bite misalignment, reducing wear on your teeth and easing discomfort in your jaw or facial muscles.

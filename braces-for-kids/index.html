Braces for Kids & Teens in Phoenix & Mesa, AZ

A beautiful smile starts with healthy development. At Arizona Orthodontics, we help children and teenagers achieve straighter teeth and healthier bites with personalized orthodontic treatment.


The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a child's first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. While not every child needs braces right away, early evaluations help identify potential concerns and determine the best time for treatment.

Braces for Kids & Teens: Modern Options for Growing Smiles

Every child has unique orthodontic needs. At our Phoenix and Mesa locations, we offer modern braces options designed to make treatment comfortable, effective, and enjoyable.

Traditional Metal Braces

A reliable and effective choice for correcting crowded teeth, gaps, and bite problems. Today's metal braces are smaller and more comfortable, and kids can personalize their smiles with colorful bands.

Charm Braces

Looking for something different? Our stylish Charm Braces offer a beautiful champagne-gold appearance, giving kids and teens a unique way to express themselves while straightening their teeth.

Customized Care for Growing Smiles

Children's teeth and jaws continue developing throughout their early years and adolescence. Our orthodontic team creates personalized treatment plans based on each child's dental development, alignment needs, and growth.


Whether your child needs early orthodontic care or comprehensive braces as a teenager, we'll help determine the right treatment at the right time.

Why Choose Braces for Your Child or Teen?

Orthodontic treatment does more than straighten teeth. It can support better oral health, improve bite alignment, and help children feel confident about their smiles.

Improved Oral Health

Straighter teeth are easier to brush and floss, helping reduce plaque buildup and the risk of cavities and gum problems.

Boosted Confidence

A beautiful smile can help children and teenagers feel more confident at school, with friends, and in everyday activities.

Better Function

Braces help correct bite problems and improve how upper and lower teeth fit together, supporting healthier chewing and tooth alignment.

Healthy Smiles Start Early

The right orthodontic care during childhood and adolescence can make a lasting difference. Whether your child is ready for braces or simply needs an early evaluation, Arizona Orthodontics makes the experience comfortable, positive, and affordable.


Our goal is to help every child grow up with a healthy smile they'll love.

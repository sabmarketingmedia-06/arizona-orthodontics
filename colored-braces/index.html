What Are Color Braces?

Color Braces are traditional metal braces enhanced with small elastic bands that come in a full spectrum of colors. These elastics are placed around each bracket, giving you the freedom to choose shades that match your personal style, mood, or special occasions. At Arizona Orthodontics, we use high-quality orthodontic materials, ensuring every treatment is efficient, precise, and comfortable while giving you endless creative possibilities.


Your braces become more than just a treatment. They become an accessory you can style your way.

Why Patients Love Color Braces

1. Fun, Personalized Expression

Your smile becomes a canvas. Choose colors that match your favorite outfits, school colors, sports teams, or holiday themes. With new color choices available at every appointment, you can refresh your look as often as you want.

2. Encouraging for Kids & Teens

Younger patients feel more excited and involved when they get to select new colors at every visit. This boosts motivation, improves cooperation, and often leads to better oral hygiene habits throughout treatment.

3. Reliable Treatment with Stylish Flair

Even though the colors add fun to the process, the orthodontic results remain serious and dependable. Color Braces are designed to fix issues like crowding, spacing, crooked teeth, and bite misalignment, delivering beautiful, healthy results guided by our experienced orthodontists.

4. Endless Color Combinations

From bold neons to soft pastels to two-tone patterns, the options are limitless. Our team helps you choose shades that complement your complexion and highlight your smile.

Your Personalized Treatment Journey at Arizona Orthodontics

Step 1: Consultation & Smile Assessment

Your journey begins with a comprehensive orthodontic evaluation. Using advanced imaging and diagnostic tools, our specialists assess your bite, alignment, and goals. We also help you pick your first custom colors for your braces.

Step 2: Placement of Your Braces

During the placement process, we ensure a smooth, comfortable experience. Once your brackets are set, you get to choose your first set of colors, marking the start of your colorful orthodontic adventure.

Step 3: Adjustments & Monthly Color Updates

At each visit, you can switch colors and try new combinations. Whether you choose seasonal colors, sports team themes, or bright hues just for fun, you can reinvent your look month after month.

Step 4: Completing Your Treatment

When your treatment ends, you'll reveal a beautifully aligned, confident smile. While the colored elastics come off, the results (and the fun memories) stay with you forever.

How to Choose the Best Colors for Your Braces

Picking colors is one of the most exciting parts of having Color Braces. Here are some simple tips:

  • Want your teeth to look whiter? Choose darker shades like navy blue, forest green, or deep purple.
  • Looking for something subtle? Try clear, silver, or soft blue bands.
  • Love standing out? Neon shades or alternating patterns are perfect for bold personalities.

Our orthodontic team will guide you toward flattering and vibrant choices that match your preferences.

How to Care for Your Color Braces

To keep your braces clean and your treatment on track:

  • Brush after every meal to avoid staining the bands.
  • Floss daily using floss threaders or a water flosser.
  • Limit staining foods like curry, dark berries, and colored beverages.
  • Avoid sticky or hard foods that can break brackets.
  • Attend all scheduled checkups for timely adjustments and fresh color selections.

Good oral care keeps your Color Braces bright and maintains steady treatment progress.

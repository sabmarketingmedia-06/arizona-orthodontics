Why Early Orthodontic Evaluation Matters?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. At this age, a skilled orthodontist for Kids can detect early signs of potential issues, such as overcrowded teeth, bite misalignment, or abnormal jaw development.


Even if no immediate treatment is needed, early evaluation allows us to monitor your child's growth and intervene at the right time. This proactive approach may include growth guidance, space maintainers, or early-phase braces to help avoid more complex treatments in the future.

Orthodontic Care for Teens

Many orthodontic treatments begin in the teen years as most permanent teeth have fully developed. Our experienced orthodontist for teens understands the unique concerns of adolescence—including aesthetics and self-esteem.


We offer a full range of teen-friendly solutions, including Invisalign—a clear aligner system designed for comfort and discretion. It's an ideal option for image-conscious teens who want effective results without traditional braces.

Braces: A Time-Tested Solution

Braces remain one of the most effective methods for correcting alignment issues such as crooked teeth, gaps, and bite problems. Today's braces are more advanced—smaller, more comfortable, and highly efficient.


Braces apply steady, gentle pressure to gradually move teeth into the correct position. They are especially effective for more complex cases that may not be suitable for clear aligners.

Invisalign: A Clear Choice for Teens

Invisalign aligners are a popular, discreet alternative to braces—perfect for teens who want an invisible treatment option. These custom-fit, removable aligners make brushing, flossing, and eating easier than ever.


Our team will evaluate whether your teen is a candidate for Invisalign, and if so, we'll design a personalized plan to ensure a smooth and effective experience from start to finish.

Why Adults Choose Orthodontic Treatment

Orthodontic care isn't just about aesthetics—it's about improving your oral health, correcting bite issues, and enhancing your quality of life. Misaligned teeth can contribute to gum disease, uneven tooth wear, jaw pain, and difficulty cleaning hard-to-reach areas.


Many adults didn't have access to orthodontic care when they were younger, while others have seen their teeth shift over time. Whatever your reason, a dentist for adults who specializes in orthodontics can help design a treatment plan that fits your lifestyle and goals.

Discreet and Effective Options: Braces & Invisalign for Adults

How Invisalign Works

Step 1: Consultation

During your initial consultation, we will create a precise treatment plan using 3D digital scans of your teeth. You'll see a preview of your new smile before you even start.

Step 2: Treatment Plan

Dr. Fernandez maps your entire Invisalign journey using iTero 3D technology — from your first aligner to your final result.

Step 3: Your Aligners

You'll receive a series of custom Invisalign aligners made just for you. You'll receive new aligners every 1-2 weeks, gently guiding your teeth into place.

Why Choose Invisalign?

  • Virtually invisible — most people won't notice you're wearing them

What Influences Your Dental Braces Cost?

Every smile is different, which means orthodontic fees can vary from patient to patient. At Arizona Orthodontics, we tailor every treatment plan to your specific needs so you always receive a personalized cost estimate. The most important factors influencing your Dental Braces Cost include:

1. Type of Braces

Today's orthodontic technology offers several options to match your comfort, style, and budget:

  • Metal Braces: Durable, reliable, and the most budget-friendly choice for patients of all ages.
  • Clear Aligners: Removable, comfortable, and nearly invisible—ideal for flexible, discreet treatment.

2. Case Complexity

The more complex your orthodontic issues—such as severe crowding, bite problems, or alignment challenges—the more time and expertise your treatment may require. This can affect your overall budget, as advanced or extended treatment often requires additional monitoring and adjustments.

3. Treatment Duration

Depending on your smile goals and current alignment, treatment may last anywhere from 12 to 24 months or more. Since all routine visits and adjustments are included in your plan, the length of your treatment plays an important role in determining your Dental Braces Cost.

4. Technology & Expertise

At Arizona Orthodontics, we use advanced digital imaging, 3D scanning, and precise treatment planning tools. These technologies allow for improved comfort, faster appointments, and more predictable outcomes—adding long-term value to your orthodontic investment.

What Are Color Braces?

Color Braces are traditional metal braces enhanced with small elastic bands that come in a full spectrum of colors. These elastics are placed around each bracket, giving you the freedom to choose shades that match your personal style, mood, or special occasions. At Arizona Orthodontics, we use high-quality orthodontic materials, ensuring every treatment is efficient, precise, and comfortable while giving you endless creative possibilities.


Your braces become more than just a treatment—they become an accessory you can style your way.

Why Patients Love Color Braces

1. Fun, Personalized Expression

Your smile becomes a canvas. Choose colors that match your favorite outfits, school colors, sports teams, or holiday themes. With new color choices available at every appointment, you can refresh your look as often as you want.

2. Encouraging for Kids & Teens

Younger patients feel more excited and involved when they get to select new colors at every visit. This boosts motivation, improves cooperation, and often leads to better oral hygiene habits throughout treatment.

3. Reliable Treatment with Stylish Flair

Even though the colors add fun to the process, the orthodontic results remain serious and dependable. Color Braces are designed to fix issues like crowding, spacing, crooked teeth, and bite misalignment—delivering beautiful, healthy results guided by our experienced orthodontists.

4. Endless Color Combinations

From bold neons to soft pastels to two-tone patterns, the options are limitless. Our team helps you choose shades that complement your complexion and highlight your smile.

Your Personalized Treatment Journey at Arizona Orthodontics

Step 1: Consultation & Smile Assessment

Your journey begins with a comprehensive orthodontic evaluation. Using advanced imaging and diagnostic tools, our specialists assess your bite, alignment, and goals. We also help you pick your first custom colors for your braces.

Step 2: Placement of Your Braces

During the placement process, we ensure a smooth, comfortable experience. Once your brackets are set, you get to choose your first set of colors—marking the start of your colorful orthodontic adventure.

Step 3: Adjustments & Monthly Color Updates

At each visit, you can switch colors and try new combinations. Whether you choose seasonal colors, sports team themes, or bright hues just for fun, you can reinvent your look month after month.

Step 4: Completing Your Treatment

When your treatment ends, you'll reveal a beautifully aligned, confident smile. While the colored elastics come off, the results—and the fun memories—stay with you forever.

How to Choose the Best Colors for Your Braces

Picking colors is one of the most exciting parts of having Color Braces. Here are some simple tips:

  • Want your teeth to look whiter? Choose darker shades like navy blue, forest green, or deep purple.
  • Looking for something subtle? Try clear, silver, or soft blue bands.
  • Love standing out? Neon shades or alternating patterns are perfect for bold personalities.

Our orthodontic team will guide you toward flattering and vibrant choices that match your preferences.

How to Care for Your Color Braces

To keep your braces clean and your treatment on track:

  • Brush after every meal to avoid staining the bands.
  • Floss daily using floss threaders or a water flosser.
  • Limit staining foods like curry, dark berries, and colored beverages.
  • Avoid sticky or hard foods that can break brackets.
  • Attend all scheduled checkups for timely adjustments and fresh color selections.

Good oral care keeps your Color Braces bright and maintains steady treatment progress.

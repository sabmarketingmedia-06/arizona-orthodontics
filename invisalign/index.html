Invisalign in Mesa & Phoenix — Straighter Teeth, No Metal

At Arizona Orthodontics, we proudly offer Invisalign® – a virtually invisible, comfortable, and convenient way to straighten your teeth. Using custom-made Invisalign aligners with SmartTrack® material, Invisalign effectively treats a range of orthodontic issues like crowding, gaps, and bite problems.

How Invisalign Works

Step 1: Consultation

During your initial consultation, we will create a precise treatment plan using 3D digital scans of your teeth. You'll see a preview of your new smile before you even start.

Step 2: Treatment Plan

Dr. Fernandez maps your entire Invisalign journey using iTero 3D technology — from your first aligner to your final result.

Step 3: Your Aligners

You'll receive a series of custom Invisalign aligners made just for you. You'll receive new aligners every 1-2 weeks, gently guiding your teeth into place.

Why Choose Invisalign?

  • Virtually invisible — most people won't notice you're wearing them
  • Removable — eat, brush, and floss normally
  • Comfortable — no metal brackets or wires
  • Brief checkups every six weeks
  • Custom Vivera® retainers to maintain your new smile after treatment

Invisalign for All Ages

Invisalign offers a great solution for anyone wanting a straighter, healthier smile without the need for metal brackets or wires. It's an option available for adults, teens, and even kids.


The aligners are removable, allowing you to eat, brush, and floss with ease, and most treatment plans involve only brief checkups every six weeks. After treatment, custom Vivera® retainers help maintain your new smile.

Meet The Doctor

Meet Dr. Alejandra Fernandez

Orthodontist located in Mesa and Phoenix AZ

"Whether you're bringing in your child or coming in yourself, I'm here to support each patient's smile transformation with care that's tailored to their needs."

Dr. Alejandra Fernandez

With over fifteen years of experience, Dr. Fernandez provides personalized orthodontic care for individuals ages 7 and older. Born in Arizona and fluent in both English and Spanish, she is dedicated to delivering care that is welcoming and patient-centered.


Outside the office, she enjoys paddleboarding, spending time outdoors with her dogs, and being with her family.

A Bit More About Dr. Alejandra Fernandez

🦷

Clinical Expertise

Dr. Fernandez offers a range of orthodontic services, including:

  • Traditional braces
  • Invisalign® clear aligners
  • Early treatment for children ages 7+
  • Comprehensive orthodontic care for teens and adults
🎓

Education and Training

Dr. Fernandez earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine from Harvard School of Dental Medicine and completed her orthodontic specialty training at the University of Puerto Rico.


Her research in craniofacial abnormalities and early orthodontic intervention has shaped her approach to creating treatment plans that support optimal function and aesthetics.


She is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists and stays up to date with the latest advancements in the field.

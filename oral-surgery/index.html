Oral Surgery Services We Offer

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-looking replacements for missing teeth. Our implants are designed to restore your smile, your bite, and your confidence — for a lifetime.

Tooth Extractions

Safe, comfortable extractions including wisdom teeth removal. We use modern techniques and sedation options to ensure a smooth, stress-free experience.

Bone Grafting

Rebuilds bone structure to support dental implants or restore jaw integrity after tooth loss. A critical foundation for long-term oral health.

Exposure of Impacted Teeth

Surgical exposure of teeth that haven't erupted properly, often coordinated with orthodontic treatment for optimal results.

Pre-Prosthetic Surgery

Prepares the mouth for dentures, bridges, or other prosthetics — ensuring a comfortable, secure fit.

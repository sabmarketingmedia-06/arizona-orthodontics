Why Adults Choose Orthodontic Treatment

Orthodontic care isn't just about aesthetics. It's about improving your oral health, correcting bite issues, and enhancing your quality of life. Misaligned teeth can contribute to gum disease, uneven tooth wear, jaw pain, and difficulty cleaning hard-to-reach areas.


Many adults didn't have access to orthodontic care when they were younger, while others have seen their teeth shift over time. Whatever your reason, a dentist for adults who specializes in orthodontics can help design a treatment plan that fits your lifestyle and goals.

Discreet and Effective Options: Braces & Invisalign for Adults

At Arizona Orthodontics, we offer modern orthodontic solutions designed with adults in mind: comfortable, efficient, and often nearly invisible.

Invisalign for Adults

One of the most popular choices for adult orthodontics is Invisalign. These custom-made, clear aligners gradually shift your teeth without the need for brackets or wires. They are virtually invisible and removable, making them perfect for busy professionals or anyone who prefers a subtle approach to treatment.


With Invisalign, you can maintain your oral hygiene routine, enjoy your favorite foods, and continue your social and professional activities with confidence.

Braces for Adults

While Invisalign is a great option for many, traditional braces may be recommended for more complex alignment issues. Today's braces are more discreet than ever, offering advanced materials that are smaller, more comfortable, and faster-acting.


As the best orthodontist for adults in the area, we'll help you determine the treatment that offers the best results for your unique needs, whether that's clear aligners or traditional braces.
